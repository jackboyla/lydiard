# Secondary coaching principles

`wisdom/magness-principles.md`. That filename is gitignored, so your own notes stay private.

A useful second source covers ground the first one leaves thin — base construction, strength work,
or event-specific preparation.

## 1. The model in one page

State the few connected parts of the approach. For example:

1. **Aerobic foundation:** sustainable easy volume, long runs, controlled sub-threshold work.
2. **Neuromuscular foundation:** strides and short, fully recovered sprints.
3. **Movement and strength foundation:** gradual load exposure and appropriate strength work.

## 2. Progressions

Describe how each part should build over a block, and what signals say it is working.

## 3. Limits

Note where the approach stops applying — training age, injury history, or current volume.
